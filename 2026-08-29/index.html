Class summary

A quick recap of what we covered and what to do next.

August 29
What we covered
  • Central Ideas and Details: separate the whole-passage idea from a specific detail, then check the scope of each answer.
  • Annotated SAT-Style Sentences: find the main clause first, then trace how each nonfinite structure connects to it.
  • We compared -ing, past participle, and to + verb structures by checking the logical subject and the relationship between the actions.
Current focus
  • Your foundation is strong. I removed the easier Central Ideas and Details questions, so the Week 1 homework uses difficult questions only.
  • For Central Ideas and Details, the main thing to check now is speed. Track how long each question takes while keeping the scope check clear.
What to improve
  • Follow the relationships inside long sentences more carefully.
  • For each supplementary structure, identify what it attaches to, what it refers back to, and what its logical subject is.
  • Then check the relationship: -ing usually signals an active result; a past participle usually signals a passive or received action; and to + verb usually signals an active purpose.
Homework
  • Complete the Week 1 homework: Central Ideas and Details + Nonfinite Modifiers (30 questions).
Suggested practice
  • Take Mock 1, the August 22 test set, to get an estimated Reading and Writing score.
  • You do not need to prioritize the official practice sets yet. They work better as full-test practice than as categorized target practice.
  • Use Skill Booster for targeted practice first. After you finish the relevant units, move on to official mock tests.
