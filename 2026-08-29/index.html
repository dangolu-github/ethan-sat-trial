Class 01 · Reading

Reading System I

Learn a clear routine for finding what a text is doing, what it is saying, and how far an answer is allowed to go.

Class focus

Read → Map → Limit → Answer

  1. Read: What job is this sentence or passage doing?
  2. Map: What are the main idea, evidence, and relationship?
  3. Limit: What scope, degree, condition, or comparison must stay intact?
  4. Answer: Which option completes the exact job without going too far?
