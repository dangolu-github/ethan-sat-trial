Systematic core

Prove the structure before choosing punctuation.

  1. Mark the subject and finite verb.
  2. Label each side as an independent clause, dependent clause, phrase, list, or continuous unit.
  3. Name the relationship across the blank.
  4. Choose only punctuation licensed by that structure.
  5. Reject the closest tempting trap by rule.

Class goal

Find the real next step

We will use exact responses—not guesses—to decide which Boundary subtype needs repair and when to move forward.

Reading Studio

Read → Map → Limit → Answer

  • Read: What job is this sentence or text doing?
  • Map: What are the claim, evidence, and relationship?
  • Limit: What scope, degree, condition, or comparison must stay intact?
  • Answer: Which option does the exact job without crossing the boundary?

Portable takeaway

Structure controls the choice.

A sentence can sound smooth and still be structurally wrong. A reading answer can sound relevant and still claim too much.
