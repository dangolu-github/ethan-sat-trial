Central Ideas and Details

  1. Read: establish the situation and any contrast.
  2. Map: identify topic, actor, action, and the passage’s “so what.”
  3. Limit: reject choices that add, distort, reverse, or overstate.
  4. Answer: choose the option that preserves the passage’s scope.

Central idea: Topic + controlling claim. Detail: actor/action + same-meaning match.

Nonfinite Modifier Decision System

  1. Recover the main-clause subject and finite verb.
  2. Name the modifier’s exact logical subject.
  3. Test whether that subject performs or receives the action.
  4. Decide purpose, result/method, passive state, or earlier completion; then protect the boundary.
