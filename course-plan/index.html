Reading & Writing · Classes 01–20

20-Week Class Plan

First, build one complete SAT question-type system at a time. Then connect those systems through systematic review, mixed reading, and timed practice.

Saturday course
01

One clear class focus

Each foundation class has one primary official question type, including a repeatable method and focused application.

02

Reading every week

Every class adds a reading move—main-idea control, evidence mapping, viewpoint tracking, dense-sentence reading, or timed selection.

03

Self-learning with a theme

Regular weeks pair a vocabulary theme with a related reading. On a mock week, the mock is the only self-learning task.

Sentence Boundaries began in Class 00.

Class 01 may finish only a small remaining point if needed. Sentence Boundaries returns later inside Systematic Review rather than as a new foundation unit.

Question-Type Foundations

Classes 01–11

The reading-heavy sequence comes first. Expression of Ideas and grammar follow once the main reading systems are in place.

Aug29

Class 01 · Information and Ideas

Central Ideas and Details

Question-type focus
Separate the passage’s central claim from true but secondary details.
Extra reading skill
Build the first Read → Map → Limit → Answer routine: topic, claim, and exact scope.
Weekly self-learning
Mock 1 only. No vocabulary set, themed reading, or additional homework.
Sep05

Class 02 · Craft and Structure

Text Structure and Purpose

Question-type focus
Translate what a sentence, paragraph, or whole passage says into the job it performs.
Extra reading skill
Track before → target → after and notice where the passage changes direction.
Weekly self-learning
Vocabulary: tone and function. Reading: literary voice and structural shifts.
Sep12

Class 03 · Information and Ideas

Inferences

Question-type focus
Choose the minimum conclusion that every part of the text can support.
Extra reading skill
Separate fact from inference and control degree, time, population, and certainty.
Weekly self-learning
Vocabulary: certainty and qualification. Reading: archaeology and incomplete evidence.
Sep19

Class 04 · Information and Ideas

Command of Evidence · Textual

Question-type focus
Match a claim to the quotation or finding that actually supports, illustrates, or weakens it.
Extra reading skill
Build a claim → evidence chain and reject evidence that is true but off-target.
Weekly self-learning
Vocabulary: claim, mechanism, and limitation. Reading: behavioral-science findings.
Sep26

Class 05 · Information and Ideas

Command of Evidence · Quantitative

Question-type focus
Connect the correct values, trend, or comparison in a graph or table to the exact claim.
Extra reading skill
Read axes, groups, units, and direction before interpreting what the data can show.
Weekly self-learning
Vocabulary: trend, comparison, and measurement. Reading: data and public-health decisions.
Oct03

Class 06 · Craft and Structure

Words in Context

Question-type focus
Predict the needed meaning, tone, and grammatical role before testing the choices.
Extra reading skill
Use local logic, contrast, cause, and register to recognize second meanings and close synonyms.
Weekly self-learning
Vocabulary: precision, tone, and word families. Reading: how meanings change over time.
Oct10

Class 07 · Craft and Structure

Cross-Text Connections

Question-type focus
Keep two authors’ claims separate before identifying agreement, disagreement, or qualification.
Extra reading skill
Use a two-column viewpoint map: position, reason, boundary, and likely response.
Weekly self-learning
Mock 2 only. No vocabulary set, themed reading, or additional homework.
Oct17

Class 08 · Expression of Ideas

Rhetorical Synthesis

Question-type focus
Read the writing goal first, filter the notes, and choose the sentence that completes every goal field.
Extra reading skill
Map research notes as aim, method, finding, limitation, and significance.
Weekly self-learning
Vocabulary: aim, method, finding, and significance. Reading: research and innovation.
Oct24

Class 09 · Expression of Ideas

Transitions

Question-type focus
Name the exact relationship between ideas before choosing a connector.
Extra reading skill
Follow argument flow through contrast, cause, example, clarification, and sequence.
Weekly self-learning
Vocabulary: contrast, cause, example, and sequence. Reading: arts and culture arguments.
Oct31

Class 10 · Standard English Conventions

Form, Structure, and Sense · Part I

Question-type focus
Control agreement, tense, finite and nonfinite verbs, and pronoun reference.
Extra reading skill
Strip a dense sentence to its subject, finite verb, time anchor, and reference chain.
Weekly self-learning
Vocabulary: reference, consistency, and sequence. Reading: dense science sentences.
Nov07

Class 11 · Standard English Conventions

Form, Structure, and Sense · Part II

Question-type focus
Control modifiers, parallelism, comparisons, possession, and number.
Extra reading skill
Track exactly what each modifier describes and which ideas share equal grammatical roles.
Weekly self-learning
Vocabulary: comparison and precision. Reading: dense technology and history sentences.

Systematic Review

Classes 12–20

The second stage does not restart the course. It connects the completed systems, repairs checked mistakes, and practices choosing the right method under mixed and timed conditions.

Nov14

Class 12 · Systematic Review I

Information and Ideas

Review focus
Connect Central Ideas, Inferences, and both Command of Evidence routes.
Extra reading skill
Move from passage claim to supported conclusion and then to the strongest proof.
Weekly self-learning
Vocabulary: qualification and causal logic. Reading: ecology research and evidence.
Nov21

Class 13 · Systematic Review II

Craft and Structure

Review focus
Connect Words in Context, Text Structure and Purpose, and Cross-Text Connections.
Extra reading skill
Track how wording, passage structure, and author viewpoint work together.
Weekly self-learning
Mock 3 only. No vocabulary set, themed reading, or additional homework.
Nov28

Class 14 · Systematic Review III

Expression of Ideas

Review focus
Review Mock 3 priorities, then connect Rhetorical Synthesis and Transitions.
Extra reading skill
Read for writing purpose: goal, audience, relationship, emphasis, and scope.
Weekly self-learning
Vocabulary: goal and relationship language. Reading: research abstracts and note sets.
Dec05

Class 15 · Systematic Review IV

Standard English Conventions

Review focus
Reconnect Sentence Boundaries with Form, Structure, and Sense through clause and controller decisions.
Extra reading skill
Read dense sentences for clause boundaries, modifier targets, reference, and logical emphasis.
Weekly self-learning
Vocabulary: reference and modification. Reading: dense historical prose.
Dec12

Class 16 · Mixed Reading Review

Precision and Trap Control

Review focus
Repair scope, degree, variable, time, and relationship traps across mixed reading questions.
Extra reading skill
Compare close answers field by field instead of relying on topic match or familiar wording.
Weekly self-learning
Vocabulary: scope and degree. Reading: competing interpretations of evidence.
Dec19

Class 17 · Subject Reading

Science, Studies, and Evidence

Review focus
Apply the completed SAT methods inside one connected science-reading theme.
Extra reading skill
Map question, method, result, limitation, and claim across related texts.
Weekly self-learning
Vocabulary: study design and evidence. Reading: one connected science theme.
Dec26

Class 18 · Timed Review

Module Strategy

Review focus
Choose the right question-type method under a realistic Reading and Writing module clock.
Extra reading skill
Use a fast first map, protect evidence-heavy decisions, and return with a clear second-pass rule.
Weekly self-learning
Vocabulary: pacing and decision language. Reading: a short mixed-domain set.
Jan02

Class 19 · Final Mixed Review

Method Selection and Readiness

Review focus
Use checked records to set final priorities across all Reading and Writing systems.
Extra reading skill
Recognize the question job quickly and select the correct reading map before comparing choices.
Weekly self-learning
Mock 4 only. No vocabulary set, themed reading, or additional homework.
Jan09

Class 20 · Course Review

Mock 4 Review and Next Plan

Review focus
Review the final mock, identify durable strengths and remaining priorities, and build the next study cycle.
Extra reading skill
Create a personal checklist for reading purpose, evidence, scope, and time pressure.
Weekly self-learning
Keep a final course word bank and a personal reading plan for the next cycle.
